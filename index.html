Markshot

Screenshot & Markup for Mac

Select. Mark up. Copy.

Press one shortcut and your screen freezes. Drag over the part you want, draw an arrow or a box right on top of it, and press Return — it is on your clipboard, ready to paste into a chat, an email or a bug report. No editor window, no files to clean up afterwards.

How it works

  • Press ⇧⌘2 anywhere, or click the Markshot icon in the menu bar. Prefer another key? Set any shortcut you like in Markshot Settings.
  • Drag to select an area. Drag the handles to adjust it, or drag inside to move it.
  • Pick a tool and draw right on the screen: pen, line, arrow, rectangle, ellipse, highlighter or text, in eight colours and three weights.
  • Press Return or ⌘C to copy, ⌘S to save as PNG or JPEG, or share with AirDrop, Messages and Mail. Esc cancels.

Made for speed

  • Lives in the menu bar and stays out of your way
  • Full Retina resolution — what you copy is pixel-sharp
  • Works across multiple displays
  • Single-key tool shortcuts, and Shift for straight lines and perfect squares
  • Your shortcut, your way — record any key combination in Settings, and reset to ⇧⌘2 with one click
  • Opens at login if you want it to
  • Nothing is uploaded — your screenshots stay on your Mac
